# DRDIP-II COMPONENT CLASSIFICATION GUIDE
# drdip-report-writer v1.0
# Development Response to Displacement Impacts Project II (DRDIP-II)
# Use this guide to assign activities to the correct DRDIP-II component.
# If an activity is not on these lists, use the decision tree at the bottom of this file.

---

## COMPONENT DEFINITIONS

| Component | Full Name |
|---|---|
| Component 1 | Social and Economic Services and Infrastructure |
| Component 2 | Sustainable Environmental Management |
| Component 3 | Livelihoods |
| Component 4 | Project Management, M&E, Capacity Building, and Coordination |

---

## COMPONENT 1 — SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC SERVICES AND INFRASTRUCTURE

Activities under this component involve building or improving physical infrastructure and service delivery for communities.

**Infrastructure sub-activities:**
- Construction of primary and secondary school classrooms
- Construction and rehabilitation of health posts and health centres
- Construction of water supply systems (boreholes, piped water schemes, hand-dug wells)
- Construction and rehabilitation of roads and access paths to communities
- Construction of market infrastructure (market sheds, slaughterhouses)
- Construction of irrigation canals and micro-irrigation schemes
- Rehabilitation of existing infrastructure under any of the above categories

**Service delivery sub-activities:**
- Provision of school furniture and equipment
- Supply of medical equipment and drugs to health facilities
- Support to community-based health workers

**Note:** O&M (operations and maintenance) activities for Component 1 infrastructure belong to Component 1, not Component 4.

---

## COMPONENT 2 — SUSTAINABLE ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT

Activities under this component involve protecting, restoring, or managing natural resources and ecosystems.

**Land and vegetation sub-activities:**
- Rangeland rehabilitation and enclosure establishment
- Afforestation and reforestation activities
- Soil and water conservation (check dams, terracing, gully treatment)
- Watershed management activities
- Area closure and communal land management

**Water and climate sub-activities:**
- Drought-resilience activities related to natural resource management
- Flood management and drainage activities
- Groundwater recharge activities

**Institutional sub-activities:**
- Formation and strengthening of natural resource management committees
- Community bylaws and regulations for environmental protection

---

## COMPONENT 3 — LIVELIHOODS

Activities under this component support income generation, economic productivity, and financial inclusion for targeted communities.

**Agricultural and pastoral sub-activities:**
- Pasture development and fodder production
- Livestock productivity improvement (veterinary services, breed improvement, feed supplementation)
- Crop demonstration plots and improved seed distribution
- Small-scale agricultural inputs (tools, seeds, fertiliser)
- Beekeeping and honey production support
- Fisheries development where applicable

**Market and enterprise sub-activities:**
- Value chain development and market linkages
- Cooperative formation, registration, and strengthening
- Small enterprise development and business skills training
- Income-generating activities for women and youth groups

**Financial inclusion sub-activities:**
- Savings and credit groups (village savings and loan associations)
- Linkage to microfinance institutions
- Financial literacy training

---

## COMPONENT 4 — PROJECT MANAGEMENT, M&E, CAPACITY BUILDING, AND COORDINATION

Activities under this component support the management, oversight, monitoring, reporting, and capacity building of the project itself.

**Project management sub-activities:**
- Preparation and review of annual workplans and budgets
- Procurement of goods, works, and services for project management
- Financial management and accounting activities
- Coordination meetings (inter-woreda, zonal, regional)
- Steering committee and technical committee meetings

**M&E sub-activities:**
- Design and updating of M&E systems, tools, and databases
- Data collection, validation, and analysis
- Preparation of progress reports and M&E reports
- Beneficiary surveys and assessments
- Environmental and social safeguards monitoring

**Capacity building sub-activities:**
- Staff training on project management, procurement, or financial management
- Training of M&E officers
- Training of community development workers
- Study tours and peer learning events for project staff

**Coordination and communication sub-activities:**
- Inter-agency coordination events
- Stakeholder engagement activities
- Grievance Redress Mechanism (GRM) operation and oversight
- Project audits and fiduciary reviews

## BOUNDARY CASES AND CROSS-COMPONENT ACTIVITIES

### Activities that span two components

| Activity | Primary Component | Secondary Component | How to Handle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Training community members on rangeland management | Component 4 (capacity building) | Component 2 (environmental management) | List under both; note the overlap |
| Establishing water user associations for irrigation | Component 1 (irrigation infrastructure) | Component 3 (livelihoods / cooperative) | List under both; note the overlap |
| Forming women's savings groups linked to livelihood inputs | Component 3 (livelihoods) | Component 4 (coordination / capacity building) | List under Component 3 primarily; note Component 4 support role |
| Environmental impact monitoring of subprojects | Component 4 (M&E / safeguards) | Component 2 (environmental management) | List under Component 4 as safeguards monitoring |

## DECISION TREE FOR AMBIGUOUS CASES

Use this tree when you cannot easily assign an activity to a component.

**Step 1:** Is the activity about building or rehabilitating physical infrastructure or improving a service delivery point?
- Yes → Component 1
- No → go to Step 2

**Step 2:** Is the activity about protecting, restoring, or managing natural resources or ecosystems?
- Yes → Component 2
- No → go to Step 3

**Step 3:** Is the activity about income generation, enterprise, agricultural production, or financial inclusion for community members?
- Yes → Component 3
- No → go to Step 4

**Step 4:** Is the activity about project management, reporting, procurement, staff capacity building, coordination, M&E, safeguards monitoring, or GRM?
- Yes → Component 4
- No → go to Step 5

**Step 5:** The activity does not clearly fit any component. Use:
`[MISSING: component assignment — please confirm which component covers this activity]`

Do not guess. Do not force an assignment. Flag it for the user.

---

## COMMON ERRORS TO AVOID

| Error | Correction |
|---|---|
| Assigning community training to Component 1 or 2 because the topic is infrastructure or environment | Community training is Component 4 (capacity building). The topic determines Component 1 or 2 only for physical construction or ecosystem work. |
| Assigning GRM to Component 1 because complaints relate to infrastructure | GRM is Component 4. |
| Assigning woreda coordination meetings to Component 1 because they discuss infrastructure | Coordination meetings are Component 4. |
| Assigning safeguards monitoring to Component 2 because it covers environmental issues | Safeguards monitoring is Component 4 (M&E / safeguards). |
